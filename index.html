Parameter & Kriteria Urgensi Visit

1. Kriteria Fasilitas / Unit

Sangat Penting: Concern 'Sangat Penting' | GPS Pelepasan/Offline/Batt Lemah | Aging Visit >21 hr (Lifetime >90) | Aging Visit >14 hr (H-3 JTO).

Penting: Concern 'Penting' | GPS Blm Lepas/Blm Pasang/Geser | Aging Visit >3 hr (OVD >3) | Aging Visit >5 hr (H-3 JTO) | Aging Visit >21 hr (Lifetime ≤90).

Moderat: Aging Visit >14 hr | Concern 'Moderat' | Aging Pasang/Ganti GPS >30 hr.

2. Kriteria Mitra / Showroom

Sangat Penting: Concern Non-Fasilitas 'Sangat Penting' | Aging Visit Mitra >60 hr.

Penting: Concern Non-Fasilitas 'Penting' | Aging Visit Mitra >30 hr.

Moderat: Concern Non-Fasilitas 'Moderat' | Aging Visit Mitra >20 hr.

Mitra Closed & Dormant: Mitra dengan produktivitas '7.Closed' dan '5. Dormant' tidak masuk kalkulasi prioritas otomatis (status Normal), kecuali jika terdapat Penugasan Concern khusus.
